Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need experience to join?
No. Most people who walk in have never used a rig or a bag. Your induction covers every zone and every machine, and the coaches on the floor are there to be asked.
Can I look around before I pay?
Yes. Book a free visit or walk in during opening hours. You can see both levels, try a station and decide afterwards.
What should I bring?
Clean indoor training shoes, a towel and a water bottle. For the combat zone, bring your own hand wraps. If you are trying it for the first time, ask at the desk about borrowing gloves.
Is there parking?
There is space for motorbikes and scooters directly in front of the building. Ask at the desk about cars.
Can I freeze or transfer my membership?
Ask at the desk. Terms depend on the plan you are on, and the staff will tell you what applies before you sign.
How do I pay?
Plans are paid at the desk. Message us on WhatsApp for the current rates and the payment options available this month.
Do you sell supplements and gear?
Yes. The shop page lists what is in stock. Order on WhatsApp and pick it up at the desk, or ask about delivery in the city.
