## Preview-resolution note

Six product files (tee, hoodie, cap, pre-workout, belt, wraps) were saved from Freepik's 626 px preview renders because the download endpoint stayed rate-limited for the whole session. Same photo, same licence, but 417–502 px square. That is sharp enough for the shop tiles at normal density and slightly soft on retina screens. When the limit clears, download the full-size files by the ids above and re-run the crop; the filenames stay the same.

## Rule for launch

Every athlete and product photo above is a stand-in. The tags on the page ("Stock photo · replace with a member") disappear when `SHOW_SAMPLES` is set to false in `js/config.js`, so do not flip that switch until the gym's own photos are in place or the Freepik credit lines are on the site.
